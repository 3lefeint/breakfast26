Create a stained glass illustration, the motif of a game achievement badge.

Style:
- the object built from a few large pieces of coloured glass, held by thick, chunky dark lead lines
- the lead lines are bold and even, no thin or fine lines, no more than about eight glass pieces
- mostly the accent colour in two or three tones, with neutral pieces in light and mid grey
- glass pieces with a gentle translucent glow inside the piece, no light rays, no halo around the object
- clear contrast between neighbouring pieces, in lightness and in hue
- viewed straight from the front
- no cast shadow, no ground plane, no window frame, no arch
- must read well on both light and dark backgrounds
- no background disc, coin, ring or badge shape behind or under the motif
