Create a plush felt toy, the motif of a game achievement badge.

Style:
- the object made of a few chunky pieces of soft felt, sewn together with thick, visible contrast stitches
- rounded, puffy forms with thick readable silhouettes, no thin fragile parts
- matte felt with a very fine fabric look, mostly the accent colour, with neutral parts in mid grey
- minimal details, at most three main parts, no tiny buttons, no loose threads, no face unless the subject asks for one
- clear contrast between neighbouring parts, in lightness and in hue
- viewed from the front
- soft key light from the upper left, shading on the object only, no cast shadow, no ground plane
- must read well on both light and dark backgrounds
- no glow, no halo
- no background disc, coin, ring or badge shape behind or under the motif
