Create a layered papercut illustration, the motif of a game achievement badge.

Style:
- the object built from at most three layers of cut paper, stacked with a small gap for depth
- bold simplified shapes with thick, readable silhouettes, no thin strips or fine cut-outs
- matte paper in flat colours without texture, mostly the accent colour, with neutral layers in mid grey
- soft shadows only between the layers, no ground plane and no cast shadow on the background
- clear contrast between neighbouring layers, in lightness and in hue
- viewed straight from the front
- must read well on both light and dark backgrounds
- no glow, no halo
- no background disc, coin, ring or badge shape behind or under the motif
